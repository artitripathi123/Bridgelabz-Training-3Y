Typography and Text Styling

Typography plays an important role in making a webpage attractive, readable, and easy to understand.

The Importance of Good Typography

Good typography improves the overall appearance of a webpage. Choosing the right font family, font size, and font weight helps users read content comfortably.

A balanced design uses highlighted text, strong text, and emphasized text to draw attention to important ideas.

Clear typography creates a better reading experience.
